# SongHub Smart Link Remote

![Android 9.0+](https://img.shields.io/badge/Android-9.0%2B-green) ![SongHub PC](https://img.shields.io/badge/SongHub%20PC-Supported-blue) ![SongHub Legacy (MegaOke)](https://img.shields.io/badge/SongHub%20Legacy%20(MegaOke)-Supported-orange?color=orange) ![Release APK](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-APK-brightgreen)

> **SongHub Smart Link Remote** is the official companion remote control application for **SongHub PC**, **Android**, and **SongHub Legacy** (formerly known as **MegaOke PC**). Transform your Android phone or tablet into a powerful karaoke remote control to search songs, manage queues, control playback, and view live synchronized lyrics right from your hands!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Universal Compatibility**: Works seamlessly with SongHub PC, Android host systems, and legacy MegaOke PC karaoke setups.
- **Complete Remote Control**: Full keypad and playback controls including Play/Pause, Stop, Next, Replay, Vocal/MPX switch, BGV, Melody, Key Pitch, Tempo, and Volume adjustments.
- **Easy Song Search & Queue**: Search your favorite songs instantly by title, artist, or song number. Reserve songs with options like *Play Now*, *Reserve 1st*, or *Add to Queue*.
- **Live Synchronized Lyrics & Screen**: View real-time scrolling lyrics, interlude animations, and karaoke score screens directly on your device.
- **Customizable Experience**: Personalize your app background wallpapers, text styles, and display settings for the ultimate karaoke night.

---

## 📱 How to Install & Use (Release APK)

1. **Download & Install**:
   - Transfer the **SongHub Smart Link Remote APK** (`app-release.apk`) to your Android phone or tablet.
   - Tap the APK file to install. (If prompted, allow installation from unknown sources in your device security settings).
2. **Connect to Your Karaoke System**:
   - Ensure your Android device and your SongHub / MegaOke PC server are connected to the same Wi-Fi or local network.
   - Open the app, enter your host server IP address in the settings, and connect.
3. **Enjoy Singing**:
   - Search for songs, add them to the queue, control the music, and sing along!
